Before you submit

Article Clinic provides AI-assisted editorial coaching based on the material you supply.

  • Protect your sources. Do not submit confidential, embargoed, source-identifying, legally sensitive or otherwise restricted material.
  • This pilot does not independently fact-check your article. It may identify claims, scenes or quotations that deserve verification.
  • If feedback says Human review required, ask an editor, lecturer or other appropriate person to check the issue.
