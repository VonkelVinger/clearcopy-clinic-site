Why ClearCopy Clinic now?

Organisations are producing more material with smaller teams, while the tools available for research, checking, translation and quality control have become dramatically more capable. CCC brings those two realities together: experienced people using modern tools carefully, so clients can recover editorial capacity without rebuilding a full in-house department.

Editorial help where quality matters

CCC is for organisations and people who publish things — not only publishers. The work can be academic, institutional, commercial, journalistic, creative or public-facing.

Editing & proofreading

Sub-editing, language editing, proofreading and publication-readiness checks for work that needs to be clear and dependable.

Translation & language

English ↔ Afrikaans translation, refinement and source-aware language work with attention to voice, terminology and context.

Research & checking

Fact-checking, source comparison, document interrogation, consistency checks and research support before publication.

Complex, urgent & high-stakes work

Long documents, multi-stage editorial projects, difficult rewrites, crisis communication and rapid-response work when an internal team is stretched.

Who we can work with

Organisations & institutions

Writers & specialists

Media & publishing teams

Tools that support editors, not replace them

CCC is also developing practical editorial tools that remove repetitive cleanup and make human review more focused.

Afrikaans Preflight

A working pilot that cleans up common mechanical problems in Afrikaans Word documents before the human edit begins. It is deliberately conservative: clear cases can be fixed automatically, while ambiguous cases are left for review.

Open Afrikaans Preflight

Protected editorial workspace — in development

The longer-term direction includes secure project tools, shared style and terminology decisions, searchable editorial memory, document preflight and quality-control utilities for clients and collaborators.

A workflow built around the work

Some jobs need a proofreader. Others need translation, research, source checking and several editorial layers. We scope the process accordingly.

Tell us what you are trying to produce

Share the format, approximate size, deadline, audience and the problem you want solved. You do not need to diagnose the editorial need yourself.

We propose the right level of intervention

That may be a straightforward edit, or a more structured workflow involving translation, research, source comparison, style decisions or multiple review stages.

People and tools do different jobs

Technology handles suitable mechanical and analytical work; experienced editors focus on meaning, judgement, accuracy, voice and exceptions.

You receive transparent, reviewable work

Changes and queries remain visible. The goal is not to take over your material, but to help you publish it with more confidence.

Pricing

Straightforward editing can often be quoted per word. More complex projects are scoped around the actual work required.

Conventional editing and proofreading commonly fall in the region of R0.20 to R0.25 per word, depending on the document, deadline and level of intervention.

Urgent and multi-stage work is quoted separately, subject to capacity.

Start with the problem, not the paperwork

You can make first contact without sending a complete document. A short description is usually enough for us to establish what we need next.

Human accountability stays in the loop
