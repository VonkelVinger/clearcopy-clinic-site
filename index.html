The person behind CCC

An editor you can talk to.

Human judgement, with technology used where it helps the work.

I’m an editor and journalist. I write regularly for Binne+Land, News24 and Netwerk24, and I’ve worked on manuscript development for publishers including Jonathan Ball.

I bring the same editorial questions to a client’s text: What are you trying to say? What does the material support? What will your reader need, and what can we make clearer?

I work with authors and people who write professionally. Depending on the project, that can mean close editing, translation, manuscript development or coaching that helps you make your own revisions.
