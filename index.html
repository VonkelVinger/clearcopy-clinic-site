Technology for leverage. People for judgement.

Clear Copy Clinic uses technology where it genuinely helps: diagnosis, comparison, repetitive cleanup, retrieval and consistency. Meaning, voice, context, difficult editorial choices and final responsibility remain human. The goal is not to make the writer disappear; it is to help the work become clearer, stronger and more trustworthy.

Human-led, AI-assisted. We may use AI to support analysis, comparison, diagnosis, drafting and consistency checks. AI does not replace human editorial judgement, and its output is not treated as an authoritative source.
