Cookies and browser storage

CCC does not use an analytics or advertising tracking cookie on these pages. The enquiry form does not save your draft enquiry in browser storage.

Campaign links

When you submit an enquiry, the form can include the source, medium and campaign name from the link you followed. It does not write those details to a tracking cookie.

Learning Lab storage

Some optional Learning Lab exercises use local storage to retain answers and an optional alias on the device. Clear these through the exercise controls or your browser settings, particularly on a shared device.

Spam prevention

The online enquiry uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check for automated abuse when you submit. It processes technical browser and connection information. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy and CCC’s privacy policy. The check is separate from marketing or advertising tracking.

Fonts and external services

CCC uses system fonts, so these pages do not request fonts from Google Fonts. Hosting, API processing and external sites have their own service and privacy policies. See the privacy policy for information about enquiries and tool processing.
