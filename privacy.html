Privacy and your material

Clear Copy Clinic is operated by Willem Kempen in South Africa. Contact willemkempen@clearcopy.clinic about your information or the handling of a project.

Enquiries and editorial work

I use the information you provide to reply, understand the brief, quote, deliver agreed work and keep necessary business records. Please include your contact details and a short description. For a book enquiry, project stage and approximate word count help me understand the brief.

You choose whether to send an enquiry. Without contact details and a brief description, I may be unable to reply or quote. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing.

The contact buttons open your email app so that you can review and send your message. Correspondence is handled through Zoho Mail and the email service you use. Hosting and service logs can contain technical connection information.

Copies of correspondence and material may remain in mailboxes, backups and necessary business records. Retention depends on the purpose, legal obligations and provider settings. We can agree particular handling or retention requirements before work begins. Please discuss restricted, confidential or sensitive material before sending it.

Book self-check

The questionnaire runs in your browser using fixed rules. It does not send answers to an AI provider. On completion, you can review and edit a summary, then copy it into an email if you choose. Your answers are shared with me only when you send them in that email. Closing or refreshing the page clears the self-check’s in-page state.

Article Clinic and Headline Clinic

These pilots send the text you submit to OpenAI through CCC’s Firebase backend to generate automated coaching. The source code disables stored response objects and does not intentionally write the source draft to an application database. This does not mean that providers retain no information: service, security and processing policies still apply.

Operational logs can record events, timings, error categories and shortened classroom-code labels. Do not submit source-identifying, embargoed, legally sensitive or otherwise restricted material. Read OpenAI’s API data controls and CCC’s AI policy.

Afrikaans Preflight and Learning Lab

Preflight sends a Word document to Firebase for mechanical processing and returns the processed file and report. That route does not use an AI provider. Keep your original and review the changes. Some optional Learning Lab exercises retain progress and an optional alias in local storage on your device until cleared.

Enquiry sources and external services

If you choose to tell me how you found CCC, I may use that information to understand which channels lead to enquiries, quotes and paid work. The contact links do not add campaign information to your email.

Firebase, Zoho, OpenAI and other providers may process information outside South Africa under their service terms and applicable safeguards. External sites have their own privacy policies. EFT payments are processed by the banks involved; payments through PayPal are subject to PayPal’s processing and privacy terms.

Your rights and complaints

Subject to the conditions in POPIA and other applicable law, you can ask whether I hold your information, request access, correction or deletion, object to processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Some information may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve a dispute.

Contact me using the email above. You may also complain to South Africa’s Information Regulator. These rights are not limited by first contacting me.
