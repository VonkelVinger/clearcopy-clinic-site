Before you submit

Headline Clinic provides AI-assisted editorial coaching. It helps you assess a headline you have written; it does not write one for you.

  • Protect your sources. Submit only material you have the right or permission to use. Do not include confidential, embargoed, source-identifying, legally sensitive or otherwise restricted material; use a generalised summary instead.
  • Your story summary and headline are sent to an external AI provider only to produce the assessment you request. ClearCopy Clinic does not intentionally store this content in an application database for this pilot. External service providers may temporarily retain data under their own service and security policies.
  • If feedback says Human review required, ask an editor, lecturer, supervisor or other appropriate person to check the issue before publication.
Headline Clinic is currently a limited pilot. If you were invited to test it, enter the access code you received. Don’t have a code? Request access.

Headline feedback

Use these lenses to review your work. Feedback will appear here after you check a headline.

Clear Copy Clinic / Headline Clinic

  • Accuracy

    Does the headline faithfully reflect what the story establishes?

  • Clarity

    Can a reader understand the headline quickly and without ambiguity?

  • Specificity

    Does it name the most useful subject, action or outcome?

  • News value

    Does it foreground what makes this story matter to the audience?

  • Style

    Does the wording fit the selected story type and intended publication?

  • Fairness and risk

    Could the wording overstate, mislead or create an avoidable risk?
