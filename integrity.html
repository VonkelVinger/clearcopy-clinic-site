Editorial practice

Academic integrity

I help students, researchers and academics improve the clarity, structure and presentation of their own work. The substance and authorship remain theirs.

Legitimate editorial support

Depending on the rules that apply, I can provide proofreading, language editing, structural feedback, formatting and editorial coaching. I can flag problems, explain unclear passages and suggest revisions for the writer to consider.

Authorship and evidence

I do not knowingly produce academic work for someone to submit as their own, invent research, fabricate sources or findings, or disguise plagiarism or other misconduct. Institutional, course, supervisor, journal and assessment rules take precedence.

AI and academic work

Tell me about restrictions on editing, AI, confidentiality, authorship and disclosure before work begins. AI output is not a research source. I check the permitted scope with you where the boundary is unclear. See how I use AI.

Similarity and AI detection

I do not guarantee a Turnitin, similarity or AI-detection score. My work supports legitimate authorship, clear writing and proper attribution.

Sources and responsibility

I may flag incomplete or inconsistent references. The client and relevant subject experts remain responsible for the underlying research, accuracy, permissions and suitability of sources, and for meeting submission requirements.

Ask about the support your project allows before sending material.
