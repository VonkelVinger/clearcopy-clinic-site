Working with Clear Copy Clinic

Agreeing the work

I confirm the scope, fee, payment currency, payment schedule and delivery date with you before work begins. An enquiry or free suitability check does not create a paid booking. Additional work requires an agreed change to scope and fee.

Book assessments and fees

The standard opening assessment is £195 for international clients, or R3,950 as a South African quote. It covers up to 10,000 words plus a synopsis, a written editorial report and a 30-minute discussion. It concerns the supplied material. A whole-manuscript assessment or line-by-line edit has a separate scope. Length, complexity, specialist material and a different brief may require an individually agreed fee.

Payment

South African clients can pay by EFT in rand. I supply bank details privately on invoices. International clients can pay through PayPal. The agreed quote and invoice confirm the payment method, currency, amount payable and any applicable taxes or agreed charges. PayPal does not accept rand as a payment currency; any conversion is agreed before booking. Conversion charges depend on your payment provider. No payment is taken through this website.

Changes and cancellation

Contact me promptly if you need to change, postpone or cancel work. Any amount due or refund will take account of work completed and unavoidable costs agreed for your booking, subject to your statutory rights. A deposit is not automatically forfeited. I will explain any proposed cancellation charge. If I cannot complete the work, I will discuss revised arrangements or refund payment for work not delivered.

Confidentiality and your material

I treat unpublished client material as confidential and use it to perform the agreed work. I do not publish it, use it as a sample or share it with an AI service without agreement. Necessary service providers process material as described in the privacy policy. Tell me about special restrictions before sending material. Your original work and rights remain yours; you confirm that you have permission to provide the material.

Reply and delivery times

The first personal reply to an enquiry is within one working day. Paid assessments and editing jobs have their own agreed delivery dates.

Editorial responsibility and limits

The author remains responsible for originality, accuracy, permissions and submission. Editing does not guarantee publication, sales, grades or a particular similarity or AI-detection result. Clear Copy Clinic is not liable for academic or institutional decisions related to submitted work, to the extent permitted by law. These terms do not exclude liability for gross negligence or remove rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Academic work and AI

Relevant institutional, employer, client and publisher requirements must be made clear before work begins. Read the academic integrity policy and AI policy.
